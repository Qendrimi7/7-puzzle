Privacy Policy

Information about how 7 Puzzle handles information and protects the privacy of children and families.

Effective Date: September 28, 2026
Effective Date: September 28, 2026

7 Puzzle (“7 Puzzle”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a collection of playful puzzle games designed primarily for children and families.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the 7 Puzzle application (the “App”).

Our approach is simple: 7 Puzzle is designed to provide its games without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information to us.

Because 7 Puzzle is designed for children, we take privacy and age-appropriate use particularly seriously.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

7 Puzzle does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the games.

We do not ask users to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Date of birth
  • Social media information
  • User-generated profiles

The App is designed to function without requiring this information.

2. Game Data and Gameplay

7 Puzzle contains several standalone puzzle experiences, including:

  • Fruit Basket
  • Rocket Shape Builder
  • Bubble Pop
  • Lost Treasures
  • Match By Shadows
  • Animal Memory Game
  • Four Piece Puzzle

The games are designed around interactions such as matching, dragging, remembering, arranging shapes, working with numbers, popping objects, and completing puzzles.

These gameplay interactions do not require users to submit personal information to us.

Where gameplay progress or preferences are stored locally on the device, that information remains on the user's device unless otherwise specifically stated within the App.

3. Purchases

7 Puzzle may offer a one-time In-App Purchase that provides access to the premium games included in the current collection, as identified in the App Store listing and purchase interface.

Important: A purchase applies to the premium games included in the collection available at the time of purchase. Future games or additional content may be offered as separate purchases and are not automatically included unless expressly stated.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit infrastructure.

We do not receive or store the user's payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Apple handles payment processing according to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Information relating to App Store purchases may be processed by Apple in order to authorize, complete, restore, or manage purchases.

For information about Apple's handling of purchase and account information, users should review Apple's applicable privacy documentation.

4. Third-Party Services

7 Puzzle may rely on platform services provided by Apple, including Apple's App Store and StoreKit functionality for In-App Purchases.

We do not use third-party services to collect children's personal information for advertising or profiling.

Any third-party service used by the production version of the App will be reviewed and reflected in this Privacy Policy and in the applicable App Store Connect privacy declarations where required.

5. Advertising and Tracking

7 Puzzle does not use advertising as part of the core game experience.

We do not use the App to build advertising profiles of children.

We do not use the App to track children across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

If advertising or tracking technology is ever introduced into the App, this Privacy Policy and the applicable App Store Connect privacy declarations will be updated before that functionality is made available.

6. Location Information

7 Puzzle does not require access to the user's precise location.

The App does not use location information as part of its puzzle gameplay.

7. Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone and Other Sensitive Information

7 Puzzle does not require access to:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos
  • Health information
  • Motion or fitness information
  • Precise location
  • Bluetooth devices

If a future version requires access to a protected system resource, the applicable permission and privacy information will be presented as required by Apple's platform rules and applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

7 Puzzle is designed primarily for children and families.

We recognize that children require additional privacy protections.

The App is designed so that children can play the games without creating an account or submitting personal information to us.

We do not knowingly use the App to collect children's names, email addresses, physical addresses, telephone numbers, precise locations, photos, videos, or other directly identifying personal information.

We also do not knowingly sell or rent children's personal information.

9. Parental Controls and Purchases

7 Puzzle may provide a parental information or parental-gate experience around areas intended for adults, including purchase-related functionality.

Purchases made through Apple's App Store are subject to Apple's purchase and account controls.

Parents and guardians should use Apple's Family Sharing, Ask to Buy, Screen Time, and other available parental controls where appropriate.

The App itself does not require a child to provide payment information.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not rent personal information.

We do not provide children's personal information to advertisers.

Because 7 Puzzle is designed without requiring users to submit personal information, we generally do not have personal information to share with third parties.

Apple may process information independently when users access Apple platform services such as the App Store and In-App Purchases. Such processing is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

11. Data Retention

Because 7 Puzzle does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to us, we do not maintain a user profile containing personal information for ordinary gameplay.

Information that is stored locally by the App remains subject to the storage and deletion controls available on the user's device.

Apple independently determines retention periods for information it processes through Apple services according to Apple's policies.

12. Data Deletion

Because 7 Puzzle does not require an account, there is generally no 7 Puzzle user account containing personal information that needs to be deleted.

If you believe that we have received personal information relating to a child or another user and would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the contact information below.

We will review the request and take appropriate action as required by applicable law.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App to protect information that may come into our possession against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

14. Links to Other Services

The App is designed to minimize external links and does not require users to visit third-party websites in order to play the games.

If future versions include external links, those links will be presented in accordance with Apple's applicable requirements for children's apps and parental controls.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, applicable laws, or platform requirements change.

When we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage parents and guardians to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
