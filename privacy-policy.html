7 Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Clear information about how 7 Puzzle handles information and protects the privacy of children and families.

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

7 Puzzle (“7 Puzzle”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a collection of playful puzzle games designed primarily for children and families.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the 7 Puzzle application (the “App”).

Our approach is simple: 7 Puzzle is designed to provide its games without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information to us.

The current version of the App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, authentication, or other third-party SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

7 Puzzle does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the games.

The App does not ask users to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Date of birth
  • Social media information
  • User profiles

The App is designed to function without requiring this information.

2. Game Data and Gameplay

7 Puzzle includes seven standalone puzzle games:

  • Fruit Basket
  • Rocket Shape Builder
  • Bubble Pop
  • Lost Treasures
  • Match By Shadows
  • Animal Memory Game
  • Four Piece Puzzle

The games include interactions such as matching, dragging, remembering, arranging shapes, working with numbers, popping objects, visual observation, and completing puzzles.

These gameplay interactions do not require users to submit personal information to us.

Where gameplay progress or preferences are stored locally on the device, that information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted by the App to us.

3. In-App Purchases

7 Puzzle includes a one-time In-App Purchase that unlocks the premium games included in the current collection, as identified in the App Store listing and purchase interface.

One-time purchase: The current purchase applies to the premium games included in the collection available at the time of purchase.

Future games or additional content may be offered as separate purchases and are not automatically included unless expressly stated.

In-App Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or store the user's payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Apple handles payment processing and related App Store account information according to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Apple may process information associated with an App Store purchase to authorize, complete, restore, or manage that purchase.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of 7 Puzzle does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking services, authentication services, crash-reporting services, or other third-party SDKs.

The App uses Apple's platform services, including StoreKit, solely for In-App Purchase functionality such as purchasing and restoring purchases.

Apple operates its own services under Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies. Information processed by Apple through Apple services is subject to Apple's practices.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of 7 Puzzle does not display third-party advertising.

The App does not use advertising SDKs or tracking technologies to build advertising profiles of children.

The App does not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

If advertising or tracking functionality is introduced in a future version, the applicable privacy disclosures and App Store Connect declarations will be updated as required before that functionality is made available.

6. Location Information

7 Puzzle does not request or require access to the user's precise location.

Location information is not used as part of the App's gameplay or core functionality.

7. Device Permissions

The current version of 7 Puzzle does not require access to:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos
  • Health or fitness information
  • Precise location
  • Bluetooth devices

If a future version requires access to a protected system resource, the applicable permission request and privacy information will be provided as required by Apple's platform rules and applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

7 Puzzle is designed primarily for children and families.

We recognize that children require appropriate privacy protections.

The App is designed so that children can play the games without creating an account or submitting personal information to us.

We do not knowingly use the App to collect children's names, email addresses, physical addresses, telephone numbers, precise locations, photos, videos, or other directly identifying personal information.

We do not knowingly sell or rent children's personal information.

9. Parental Controls and Purchases

Purchase-related functionality is intended for use by parents or guardians.

Purchases made through Apple's App Store are subject to Apple's purchase and account controls.

Parents and guardians can use Apple's available parental controls, such as Family Sharing, Ask to Buy, and Screen Time, where appropriate.

The App itself does not require a child to provide payment information.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not rent personal information.

We do not provide children's personal information to advertisers.

Because the App does not require users to submit personal information to us, we generally do not have personal information collected through ordinary gameplay to share with third parties.

Apple may independently process information when users access Apple platform services such as the App Store and In-App Purchases. That processing is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

11. Data Retention

Because 7 Puzzle does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to us, we do not maintain a user profile containing personal information for ordinary gameplay.

Information stored locally by the App remains on the user's device and is subject to the storage and deletion controls available on that device.

Apple independently determines retention periods for information processed through Apple's services according to Apple's policies.

12. Data Deletion

Because 7 Puzzle does not require an account, there is generally no 7 Puzzle user account containing personal information that needs to be deleted.

If you believe that we have received personal information relating to a child or another user and would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the contact information below.

We will review the request and take appropriate action as required by applicable law.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App to protect information that may come into our possession against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

14. Links to Other Services

The App does not require users to visit third-party websites in order to play the games.

This website may contain links to the 7 Puzzle Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and other informational resources.

If future versions of the App include external links, those links will be presented in accordance with Apple's applicable requirements for children's apps and parental controls.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our information practices, applicable laws, or platform requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
